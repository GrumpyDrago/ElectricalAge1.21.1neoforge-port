## Thermal Camera

<ItemImage id="eln:thermal_camera" />

Hold it to see how hot cables and machines are.  
Hot spots show long before anything burns.  
Range: 24 blocks  

More: [instruments](instruments.md).

## Lockout Padlock

<ItemImage id="eln:lockout_padlock" />

Use it on a breaker, switch or breaker box to lock  
it open with your name on it. Nobody can switch  
it on until every lock is off; only you can take  
yours off, by using a padlock on it again.  

More: [breaker boxes](breaker-boxes.md).

## SCADA Linker

<ItemImage id="eln:scada_linker" />
