## Oscilloscope

Plots two voltages over time on its screen: channel 1 (yellow) on its front, channel 2 (blue) on its back. Probe power
or signal cables; for a current, probe the output of a current sensor. The time across the screen goes from 6 seconds
to 5 minutes, and **Hold** freezes the picture. Use it to watch a relay trip, a battery sag or a turbine spin up.

## Thermal camera

Hold it to see how hot every part within 24 blocks is, cables included, from dark blue (ambient) through red and
yellow to white. The hottest is outlined, and a scale shows on the right. Hot spots show long before anything burns:
look for them before you add more load.
