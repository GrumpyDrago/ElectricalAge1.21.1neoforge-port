# Electrical Age

Electrical Age simulates electricity: every cable, battery and machine has a voltage and a current, and the power
goes where the circuit sends it, not where you wish it would. This guide covers the basics and the newer parts:
batteries, protection, breaker boxes, the grid and its substations, renewable power, instruments and computers.

Hold the guide key over an item of the mod to jump to its page.

## Where to start

- [The basics](basics.md): voltage, current, power, and why there are four voltages.
- [Batteries](batteries.md): chemistries, wear, and what to do when one overheats.
- [Protection](protection.md): relays, battery management, transfer switches and arc flash.
- [Breaker boxes](breaker-boxes.md): eight circuits in one cable, and lockout.
- [The grid](grid.md): transmission towers, utility poles and substations.
- [Solar and wind](renewables.md): getting the most out of panels and turbines.
- [Instruments](instruments.md): the oscilloscope and the thermal camera.
- [Computers](computers.md): reading and switching everything from CC: Tweaked.
- [SCADA](scada.md): a control room screen for your whole power system.
