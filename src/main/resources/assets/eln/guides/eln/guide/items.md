# All items

Every item of the mod, by creative tab, with its picture and description.

- [Power and cables](items-power.md): cables, generators, transformers, batteries and switching.
- [Signals and sensors](items-signals.md): signal cables, sensors, logic and control.
- [Lighting](items-lighting.md): lamps, sockets and their supplies.
- [Machines](items-machines.md): machines that make, heat, mine and process.
- [Tools and armor](items-tools.md): meters, brushes and other tools.
- [Ores and materials](items-materials.md): ores, dusts, ingots and parts for crafting.
- [Creative](items-creative.md): parts for creative mode and testing.
