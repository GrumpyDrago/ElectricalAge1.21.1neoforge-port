## Overcurrent Protection Relay

<ItemImage id="eln:overcurrent_protection_relay" />

A breaker driven by an overcurrent relay, as on power lines.  
Trips on inverse-time curves, at once on a short circuit,  
and can close again by itself a few times.  
Put a cable in it to rate the contacts.  
Left: closed signal output. Right: remote trip input.  

More: [protection](protection.md).

## Battery Management System

<ItemImage id="eln:battery_management_system" />

Goes between a battery and what it powers.  
Cuts the load off before the battery is flat,  
stops charging when it is full,  
and opens on overcurrent.  
Front: battery. Back: load and charger. Left: charge signal.  
Put a cable in it to rate the contacts.  

More: [protection](protection.md).

## Automatic Transfer Switch

<ItemImage id="eln:automatic_transfer_switch" />

Feeds a load from the mains, and switches it  
to a backup source when the mains fail.  
With a battery and a charger it makes a UPS.  
Front: load. Back: mains. Left: backup. Right: on-backup signal.  
Put a cable in it to rate the contacts.  

More: [protection](protection.md).

## 200V Breaker Box

<ItemImage id="eln:200v_breaker_box" />

Eight circuit breakers that feed the lines  
of a power bundle cable from one supply.  
Front: supply. Back: power bundle cable.  
Nominal voltage: 200V  
Breakers from 1.00 to 10.0 A; feed it with a supply cable.  
Each trips on a lasting overload or at once on a short circuit.  
Breaking a short circuit can make an arc flash.

<ItemImage id="eln:mppt_charge_controller" />

Charges a battery from solar panels or a wind turbine  
at their maximum power point, whatever the battery voltage.  
Front: + of the panels or turbine. Back: + of the battery.  
Right: common negative, for the - of both. Left: power signal.  
Maximum input voltage:  300V  
Maximum output current:  60.0A  
Efficiency:  97.5%
